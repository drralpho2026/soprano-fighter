# Soprano Fighter

A landscape iPhone fighting game in the style of Street Fighter, with six fighters from *The Sopranos*.

**Play it here:** [https://drralpho2026.github.io/soprano-fighter/](https://drralpho2026.github.io/soprano-fighter/)

## How to play

Best of 3 rounds. After the intro, pick your fighter and a stage (the Bada Bing parking lot at night, or Satriale's at dusk).

- Walk, crouch, jump, and hold back to block
- Light and heavy attacks, combos, throws, and knockdowns
- Specials with a joystick motion or the SPECIAL button
- SUPER and ULTRA meters fill as you land hits
